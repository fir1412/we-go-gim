Name: we go gim

Short description (under80characters):
Your next workout, filled in. Log sets and track progress. Offline, no account.

Full description:

Open your workout and your next weights and reps are already there, based on your last session.
Tick each set, take your rest, and keep going.

- Track weights, reps, warm-ups, supersets and notes.
- Switch kg, lb, machine levels or bodyweight during a workout. Earlier completed sets stay as logged.
- See your workout history, progress trends and muscle levels.
- Build a weekly programme or choose a ready-made plan.
- Use optional Android rest notifications when the screen is off.
- Set optional training-day reminders, or edit and share a gym reminder in your own words.
- Import workout logs and keep your own JSON backups or CSV exports.
- Works offline for daily tracking. No account, no ads, no subscription.
- English, Bahasa Melayu, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Japanese.

Your workouts and body data stay on your device. Backups go only where you choose to save or share
them. Optional feedback sends only your message, optional contact and basic app information.
PDF import needs internet to download its reader on first use.

Rest notifications need your permission. Precise alerts depend on Android alarm access; battery
restrictions and Do Not Disturb may delay or silence them. Screen illumination depends on your phone.

Moving from the website app? Save a JSON backup there, then restore it in the Android app.
The website and Android app have separate local storage.
Android workout data uses app-private storage, separate from browser data. Uninstalling the app
or clearing its Android storage erases it; save exported backups for recovery.
Training reminders schedule the next four weeks each time you open the app.

Fitness tracking and training suggestions, not medical advice.
