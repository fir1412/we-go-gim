VOICE PROFILE
=============
Author: we go gim product copy (not a personal writing fingerprint)
Goal: useful workout reminders that sound like the existing app.
Confidence: high for product tone; draft copy still benefits from user review.

Source Set
- README: "so you never copy and paste a workout again".
- Welcome: "Every workout, filled in for you".
- Workout: "Enter the weight first" and "Tick at least one working set".
- Today: "Train anyway" and missed-day choices.
- Settings: "Keep screen on during workouts".
- Backup: "Your data is still on this phone".
- Timer: "Rest done. Next set."
- Privacy: "backups go only where you send them".

Rhythm
- One or two short sentences. Concrete words, easy to scan on a lock screen.

Compression
- One useful next step. No motivational speech or scientific lecture.

Capitalization
- Sentence case. Preserve the product name "we go gim".

Parentheticals
- Avoid in notifications. Explain limitations in Settings instead.

Question Use
- Rare. Use an invitation rather than an obligation or a bait question.

Claim Style
- Describe things the app actually does: show a plan, log a set, save a note.
- Do not claim the user missed a workout, slept badly, or made progress without evidence.
- No promises about health, strength, weight loss or injury prevention.

Preferred Moves
- Respect shorter sessions, changed plans, rest and personal choice.
- Give a small practical action: check the plan, set a time, prepare a bag, log reps.
- Keep private weight, body, pain and gym details off lock screens by default.

Banned Moves
- Guilt, shame, threats to streaks, body judgments, "no excuses", "earn your food".
- Hype, fake urgency, medical advice, pushing through pain, guaranteed outcomes.
- "not X, just Y", engagement bait, generic slogans and sales language.

CTA Rules
- Optional invitation. Never imply that exercising now is required.
- Notifications open Workout; editable messages are shared only after the user chooses.

Channel Notes
- Android: short body, generic product title; opt-in and one reminder per planned day.
- Share draft: user can edit, copy or choose an app. No automatic sending or app-link insertion.
- Settings: explain timing, permissions and limits before the opt-in control.

Production notes
- Brand-voice SKILL.md used. Dedicated UX-writing/microcopy skills were not installed
  in the available catalogs or searched local skill directories; plain-language, action-first,
  respectful microcopy practices applied directly.
- 1,000 English drafts are composed from curated compatible blocks: 20 themes ×10openings×5closings.
  The complete outputs are persisted, deduplicated and length/wording checked. These are draft
  alternatives, not 1,000 independent clinical or behavioural recommendations.
